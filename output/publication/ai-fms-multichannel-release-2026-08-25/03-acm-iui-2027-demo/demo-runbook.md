# AI-FMS Live Demo Runbook

## Demo objective

In four minutes, show that AI-FMS is an intelligent review interface rather than a score generator. The audience should leave understanding three things: the evidence is inspectable, the reviewer remains in control, and the system can abstain.

## Required equipment

- Laptop with power adapter.
- Chrome or another tested Chromium browser.
- Local production build and local API/data bundle.
- Permission-cleared demonstration videos preloaded locally.
- Offline backup screen recording.
- HDMI/USB-C display adapter.
- Optional second display for presenter notes.

Do not depend on venue Wi-Fi.

## Four-minute sequence

### 0:00-0:30 - Problem and record

Open the main Workbench on a permission-cleared Deep Squat repetition. Explain that the unit of review is a traceable repetition, not a whole unstructured video.

### 0:30-1:10 - Segmentation and playback

Show the analysis range, repetition list, loop playback, and manual boundary adjustment. Keep the video playing while speaking.

### 1:10-1:55 - Inspectable evidence

Enable pose overlay. Open quantitative evidence and point to two values on the video. Explain that the reviewer can see whether landmarks correspond to the body.

### 1:55-2:30 - Human correction

Change camera view, side, or protocol condition. Show that evidence and suggestion update. Edit the final score without accepting the AI suggestion.

### 2:30-3:05 - Abstention

Open a prepared staged Deep Squat or unresolved Rotary Stability example. Show the abstention reason and requested human confirmation. Emphasize that insufficient evidence is different from a low score.

### 3:05-3:35 - Blind study and adjudication

Switch to Study Mode. Point out that filenames, AI output, pose evidence, prior answers, and the other reviewer are hidden. Then show the adjudication or export view.

### 3:35-4:00 - Same score, richer evidence

Show one same-score Deep Squat or Hurdle Step comparison. Close with: AI organizes and quantifies evidence; the reviewer owns protocol and judgment.

## Prepared demo cases

- **Case A:** clean Deep Squat with visible overlay and complete evidence.
- **Case B:** same-score comparison showing a different movement strategy.
- **Case C:** staged protocol or ambiguous cycle that triggers abstention.
- **Case D:** blind Study Mode item with no identifying filename.

## Recovery paths

- If pose rendering fails: switch to the precomputed evidence record and continue.
- If the local API fails: use the static production demo.
- If video playback fails: play the offline backup recording and narrate live.
- If the projector is low resolution: zoom the browser to the prepared presentation setting and use the evidence-detail view.

## Privacy reset before each session

- Clear browser history and recent-file lists.
- Confirm no local paths or subject names are visible.
- Use only the public demo database.
- Disable notifications.
- Close private tabs and terminal windows.
