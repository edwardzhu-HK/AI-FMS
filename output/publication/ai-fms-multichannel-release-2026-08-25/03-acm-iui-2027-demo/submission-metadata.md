## Title

AI-FMS: An Explainable Human-in-the-Loop Interface for Functional Movement Screen Video Review

## Short abstract for PCS

AI-FMS is a browser-based human-in-the-loop interface for reviewing all seven Functional Movement Screen movements from video. It combines repetition segmentation, looped playback, pose overlays, movement-specific quantitative evidence, explainable first-pass score suggestions, quality warnings, protocol-aware abstention, independent review, adjudication, and traceable export. A four-movement Phase I internal evaluation used 32 repetitions and two reviewers across two blind rounds. The demo emphasizes how users inspect, correct, or reject AI suggestions rather than autonomous scoring. AI-FMS supports movement-screening review and research workflow; it is not a diagnostic or injury-prediction system.
