## 1. 视频定位

这不是单纯的 software demo。它同时承担两项任务：

1. **展示项目**：AI-FMS 的问题、功能、研究方法、发现和边界。
2. **展示申请人**：Ronnie 的专业准备、表达能力、研究动机、实际角色、反思和未来方向。

因此正式视频应满足：

- Ronnie 全程担任 narrator；
- 开头和结尾必须本人正面出镜；
- 中间从 FMS 实践转入 AI-FMS 时，建议再短暂出镜一次；
- screen recording、证书、FMS 实践和研究图只作为支撑 Ronnie 叙事的 B-roll；
- 不把视频录成逐个按钮教学，也不按论文目录朗读。

## 3. 故事结构

1. **Who I am**：Ronnie、游泳经历与动作质量兴趣。
2. **What FMS is**：七个 fundamental movement patterns、0-3 ordinal score、screen
   而不是 diagnosis。
3. **Why I was prepared**：完成 FMS Level 1 和 Level 2 training/certification。
4. **What I observed in practice**：实际评估中发现回放、远程审核、定量观察和信息压缩问题。
5. **What I built and studied**：AI-FMS 七动作系统与四动作 Phase I 评估。
6. **What I learned**：human oversight、data quality、abstention、research responsibility。
7. **Where I want to go**：大学阶段继续探索 Human Movement Science、biomechanics
   和 responsible AI。

## 4. 录制素材

### A-roll：Ronnie 出镜

- 开场 medium close-up，约 18 秒。

- AI-FMS Workbench 真实视频与 pose overlay。
- Study Mode dry-run 匿名盲评界面。
- Phase I 核心数字卡和四张 movement-specific figures。
- 简洁 end card：项目名、Ronnie 姓名和最终有效链接。

### 权利与隐私

- 证书画面只显示经批准的姓名、课程名称、级别和日期；遮挡 certificate number、QR
  code、email、住址或不必要的 identifier。

- FMS 实践和动作视频只使用取得展示同意的素材。
- 不显示真实 participant 信息、private reviewer comments、本地路径、通知或文件名分数提示。

## 5. Master Script：约 4 分 25 秒

### 0:00-0:18 | 本人出镜：个人起点

**画面**：Ronnie 正面出镜。背景简洁，最好能自然看见 FMS kit 或运动环境，但不要堆满道具。

**English narration**：

> Hi, I'm Ronnie. Years of swimming taught me to notice how small differences in
> movement can affect control, efficiency, and performance. That curiosity led me
> to study the Functional Movement Screen, and eventually to build AI-FMS.

**表达重点**：直接看镜头，不先放 logo，不用宏大口号；第一句先建立“人”。

### 0:18-0:42 | FMS 是什么

**画面**：Ronnie 继续出镜 2-3 秒，然后切七动作 B-roll。屏幕可依次显示：Deep Squat、
Hurdle Step、In-Line Lunge、Shoulder Mobility、ASLR、Trunk Stability Push-Up、
Rotary Stability。

**English narration**：

> FMS stands for Functional Movement Screen. It is a standardized screening
> system that uses seven fundamental movement patterns to observe how mobility,
> stability, symmetry, and motor control work together. Each pattern is scored
> from zero to three. It is a movement screen, not a medical diagnosis.

**内容边界**：不要在这里讨论 injury prediction，也不要把 FMS 说成疾病诊断或完整体能测试。

### 0:42-1:02 | 培训与 Level 1 / Level 2 Certification

**画面**：Ronnie 出镜一句后，分别展示 Level 1 和 Level 2 certificate。每张至少停留
2.5-3 秒，让观众能看清姓名和级别；可叠加简洁 lower third。

**English narration**：

> Before evaluating other people, I wanted to understand the protocol
> responsibly. I completed FMS Level 1 and Level 2 training and earned both
> certifications. The training gave me a structured foundation for setting up
> the seven screens, applying the scoring rules, and recognizing when a result
> requires further human attention.

**表达边界**：认证说明 Ronnie 受过相关训练，不自动等于 clinician、medical expert 或
research validation。

### 1:02-1:30 | 实践中发现问题，并转入 AI-FMS

**画面**：FMS kit 设置、Ronnie 说明动作、观察或记录评分；最后 6-8 秒回到 Ronnie
出镜，讲出转折句。

**English narration**：

> After the training, I began conducting and reviewing FMS assessments. I saw
> four practical limitations. Movements pass quickly; remote review makes
> repetitions slow to find; visual judgment remains qualitative even when angles
> and trajectories matter; and the zero-to-three score preserves little of the
> evidence behind the decision. Those problems became the starting point for
> AI-FMS.

**转场**：最后一句由 Ronnie 对镜头说完，然后切入完整 Workbench。

### 1:30-1:50 | 七动作系统范围

**画面**：AI-FMS Workbench 全景；展开 action selector，快速扫过七个动作。

**English narration**：

> AI-FMS is an explainable, human-in-the-loop video-review system. The workbench
> supports all seven FMS movements, with repetition-level review and a
> movement-specific, pose-based first-pass suggestion. The AI can explain its
> evidence and abstain when video quality or protocol information is incomplete.

### 1:50-2:18 | 真实视频、rep 与 pose evidence

**画面**：播放一小段 Deep Squat，暂停在最低位；打开真实 skeleton；点击相邻 reps；
展示 loop playback 和 segment timing。

**English narration**：

> A reviewer can isolate complete repetitions, replay a difficult moment, and
> correct the timing instead of searching through the full video again. The
> MediaPipe overlay is aligned to the video, allowing the system to preserve
> angles, normalized distances, trajectories, and movement events that are hard
> to record consistently by eye.

### 2:18-2:42 | AI evidence 与人工最终判断

**画面**：feature panel、AI suggestion、protocol condition 和 reviewer form。鼠标只做
一到两个有意义的动作，不快速点击。

**English narration**：

> The AI suggestion supports, rather than replaces, reviewer judgment. It shows
> which criteria support a score, what remains uncertain, and whether protocol
> information is missing. The reviewer records the final score, confidence,
> camera view, side, clearing information, and notes.

### 2:42-3:02 | Study Mode 与盲评边界

**画面**：切到 Study Mode dry-run，显示匿名队列、动作视频和 RAW SCORE controls。

**English narration**：

> To evaluate the system without leaking AI information, we built a separate
> Study Mode. In both blinded rounds, reviewers could not see AI scores, pose
> parameters, source file names, previous answers, or the other reviewer's
> decisions. Reviews were stored as append-only events and exported with
> checksums.

### 3:02-3:28 | Phase I 评估

**画面**：简洁 results card。突出 `110 reps`、`32 blind-reviewed`、`26/26 human
exact`、`16/25 AI exact`、`23/25 within one`，不要滚动整篇论文。

**English narration**：

> Phase I reconstructed 110 repetitions from 28 source videos and selected 32
> across four movements for two blinded rounds. In Round B, the reviewers agreed
> on scoreability for all 32 items and assigned the same score to all 26 jointly
> scorable items. The locked AI exactly matched human consensus on 16 of 25
> comparable items and was within one point on 23. This is an internal benchmark,
> not held-out clinical validation.

### 3:28-3:50 | 系统带来的科研发现

**画面**：四张 research figures 依次显示，每张约 5 秒。

**English narration**：

> The quantitative evidence also revealed what the ordinal score does not show.
> Deep Squat formed a strategy continuum; ASLR preserved side and repeatability
> differences; Hurdle Step showed several pathways to the same score; and Rotary
> Stability required a full-cycle view of coordination and event sequence.

### 3:50-4:28 | 本人出镜：角色、反思与未来

**画面**：回到 Ronnie 正面出镜。结尾 3-4 秒淡入简洁 end card。

**English narration**：

> My role in this project grew far beyond demonstrating a piece of software. I
> learned the FMS protocol, completed the blinded reviews, helped shape the study
> design, and interpreted both the useful evidence and the system's failures. I
> worked through an AI-assisted development process, but the project taught me
> that responsible technology depends on human judgment, data quality, and the
> willingness to say when the evidence is not enough.
>
> In college, I hope to continue exploring Human Movement Science, biomechanics,
> and responsible AI. I would like to test AI-FMS on new held-out videos, involve
> more trained reviewers, and study whether these quantitative movement profiles
> can guide better follow-up assessment without turning a screen into a
> diagnosis. For me, AI-FMS is not the end of a project. It is the beginning of a
> research direction I want to keep pursuing.

**最后画面**：

- `AI-FMS`
- `Ronnie [正式英文姓名]`
- `Human Movement Science · Responsible AI · Movement Evidence`
- 最终有效的 project page / GitHub link；链接未完成时不要显示 placeholder。

## 8. Presentation 设计

### 出镜

- 镜头与眼睛同高，medium close-up，构图留出少量字幕空间。
- 使用稳定主光和清晰收音；音质优先于复杂运镜。
- 背景简洁、有真实运动或 FMS 语境，但不要做成道具陈列。
- 开头语速略慢，结尾直视镜头；避免明显逐字读 teleprompter。
- 中段可以使用画外音，但全部 narration 必须由 Ronnie 本人录制。

### 剪辑

- A-roll 切 B-roll 时保留 Ronnie 的声音连续性。
- 证书、FMS 实践、系统界面和 research figures 都应服务于正在说的句子。
- 不使用过度动画、快速 zoom、模板化转场或持续背景音乐压过人声。
- 建议 1080p 或更高、16:9、英文主字幕；字幕需人工逐句校对。
