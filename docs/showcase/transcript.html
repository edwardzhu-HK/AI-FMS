00:00:06 AI-FMS is an explainable, human-in-the-loop video-review system.

00:00:11 The workbench supports all seven FMS movements, with repetition- level review

00:00:17 and a movement-specific, pose-based first-pass suggestion.

00:00:20 The AI can explain its evidence and abstain when video quality or protocol information is incomplete.

00:00:26 A reviewer can isolate complete repetitions, replay a difficult moment, and correct the timing

00:00:36 instead of searching through the full video again. The MediaPipe overlay is aligned to the video,

00:00:42 allowing the system to preserve angles, normalized distances, trajectories, and movement events that are hard to record consistently by eye.

00:00:50 The AI suggestion supports, rather than replaces, reviewer judgment.

00:00:54 It shows which criteria support a score, what remains uncertain,

00:01:00 and whether protocol information is missing.
